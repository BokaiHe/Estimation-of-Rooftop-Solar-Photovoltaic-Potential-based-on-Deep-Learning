# Estimation of Rooftop Solar Photovoltaic Potential based on Deep Learning
In this project, Machine learning techniques were utilized  to determine the suitability of rooftops in New York for solar panel installation, thereby promoting environmental sustainability. Specifically, a semantic segmentation model was trained using RGB and Near-Infrared (NIR) data with a U-Net architecture to create a mask that identifies rooftop areas. This mask serves as a foundational step in identifying rooftops with significant potential for renewable energy production. The U-Net model, known for its effectiveness in semantic segmentation tasks, demonstrated high accuracy in distinguishing between rooftops and other urban features, ensuring precise identification of suitable areas.
